01 — ABOUT

MORE THAN JUST CODE

Turning everyday problems into simple ideas.

CodeForge started with a simple idea: technology becomes more interesting when people bring different strengths to the same table.

Instead of trying to be experts in everything, we learn from each other. One person might think about code, another about design and communication, while another sees the practical side of a problem.

That difference is what makes CodeForge what it is.

PROJECT 02 / EDUCATION

Student Study Planner

A proposed educational planning system for Navjeevan Education Society, designed around the everyday needs of both teachers and students.

The concept focuses on helping students organise their studies, follow academic progress, manage tasks and build better consistency. Teachers can use the system to structure academic activities and provide students with clearer direction.
